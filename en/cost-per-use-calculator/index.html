Divide the purchase price by the number of days or uses. This estimate leaves out repairs and resale value. The calculator runs in your browser and does not send your entries.

How much will a winter coat cost per wear?

Use the winters you can realistically count on. A $180 coat worn twice a week for 12 weeks comes to $7.50 per wear in the first winter. If you wear it only 10 times, the cost rises to $18 per wear.

To reach $3 per wear, the $180 coat needs 60 wears. At 24 wears a winter, you’d reach that point in the third winter. Try your own price and count how often you wore a similar coat last winter.

Which number should you use?

Cost per use

A $300 camera lens used on 20 outings costs $15 per outing. If you take it out just 8 times, that rises to $37.50. Use this measure for gear with a countable use, such as a camera lens or a pair of running shoes.

Cost per day

A $900 laptop kept for 1,000 days costs $0.90 per day. This measure works for things you keep and use regularly, such as a desk or laptop.

See how close your estimate was

If you buy the item, add it to Valuday and log each wear or use. Its cost per use updates as you use it, so you can compare the result with your estimate. Still deciding? Save the purchase to your waiting list and revisit it later.
