Photo Shooting Days Counter

Pick JPEG photos to see how many different days you took pictures. A whole burst of photos on the same date counts as one shooting day.

Select one or more .jpg or .jpeg files. Your photos stay on this device.

How shooting days are counted

The counter reads the original capture date (DateTimeOriginal) stored in each JPEG’s EXIF metadata. Ten photos taken on May 3 and one photo on May 4 count as two shooting days. The counter keeps the calendar date recorded by the camera, with no time zone conversion.

Why a file might be missing a date

Apps sometimes remove capture dates when sharing or exporting photos. The counter leaves missing dates out of the total. Last-modified dates can change when files are copied. Select JPEG originals or full JPEG copies that retain capture metadata. RAW, HEIC, and PNG files aren’t supported.
