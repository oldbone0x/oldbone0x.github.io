How to count unique photo dates

Pick one or more original JPEG photos. The counter extracts the DateTimeOriginal field from each file’s EXIF metadata, groups identical calendar dates, and counts each date once. Ten images from May 3 and one from May 4 count as two shooting days.

What about missing dates, HEIC, and time zones?

Some apps remove EXIF data when exporting or sharing photos. We exclude files without readable dates rather than treating their file modification time as the capture time. The date is taken exactly as the camera recorded it, without time zone conversion. This version reads JPEG metadata only; convert HEIC or RAW to JPEG while keeping EXIF dates before selecting them.
