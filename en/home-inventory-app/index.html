StoreWhere Home Inventory

Find the drawer where you put it.

Keep a record of what is in each room, cabinet, drawer, and storage bin. Search for a spare charger and see where you put it and how many you have left.

Available on the U.S. App Store as 有处：家庭物品收纳. The app interface is currently in Simplified Chinese.

View in the U.S. App StoreSee how to get started

What you can do

Organize by storage location

Create a location such as Study → Desk → Top drawer. Add several items at once, or keep a photo of what is inside.

Search items and update quantities

Search by item name to see its full location. Adjust the quantity as you use or replace items.

Review photo suggestions before saving

Optional AI can suggest item names, quantities, and image crops from a storage photo. Review the suggestions and make any corrections before saving.

Getting started

  1. Add one place you often search, such as the hallway cabinet.
  2. Create a location for each shelf or bin. Add items manually or review suggestions from a photo.
  3. Search for an item when you need it. Update its quantity when you take or replace one.

Common questions

Can I use StoreWhere without an internet connection?
You can browse existing inventory, add items manually, and search by text offline. Optional AI photo recognition needs a connection.
Does StoreWhere upload my photos?
Manually organized records are kept on your device and can sync through iCloud if enabled. If you confirm an AI photo recognition request, the selected image is sent to a third-party AI service for processing. See the privacy policy for details.
Is the iPhone or iPad app available in English?
The current App Store release lists Simplified Chinese as its interface language.
